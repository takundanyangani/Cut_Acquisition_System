Rules every route follows

  • Nobody can act on a request they raised, whatever roles they hold.
  • Any reviewer can return a request for changes, with a reason. It comes back to the same reviewer after you resubmit.
  • Only the ICT Director can decline a request.
  • An officer who will be away can delegate their role for a set period. The acting officer's signature shows both names.
  • Each stage has a target in working days, listed below. Weekends and public holidays do not count. A request past its target sends a daily reminder to whoever holds it.
  • Every hand-over of equipment uses an issue voucher (CUT ICT 40). You confirm the serial number, specification and condition before you sign.
